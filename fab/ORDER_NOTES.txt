Notes for the JLC engineer (paste into the order remark)
  - Decorative business card. All SMD pads are unconnected and have no net. This is intentional.
  - The back has one intentional 0.10 mm copper gap: the "<=30 V / 0.1" clearance demo
    (two 1.6 x 0.8 mm bars). Please keep it as drawn. The pair shares one solder-mask
    opening, because a 0.1 mm mask web is not manufacturable.
  - Large exposed copper (ENIG) areas with mask openings are intentional (rulers, combs, lines, captions).
  - All holes are plated. The small holes are a wire-gauge demo, so a tight tolerance is welcome.
